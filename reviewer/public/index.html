SkoolBox is building Martha, an offline AI tutor for Nigerian classrooms. Before any of this material reaches a pupil, a teacher who knows the subject needs to confirm it is actually correct. That is what we are asking of you.

Please read this part. The answers below were drafted by AI against the NERDC curriculum. They have not been checked by a teacher. Assume nothing is correct until you have read it — that scepticism is the whole point of your review.

What to look for

  • Correct. The facts hold, and every step of any working actually adds up.
  • On the syllabus. The topic belongs to NERDC / WAEC / NECO for that class.
  • Pitched right. The language and depth suit the stated class level.
  • Shows the working. Solve-type answers walk through it, not just the result.
  • Nigerian. Naira, local names and places, used naturally rather than sprinkled on.
  • Sounds like a teacher. Warm and clear, never robotic.
  • Appropriate. Nothing biased, harmful or off-topic for a child.

If an answer fails on any of these, mark it Needs correction and tell us why in a line. A specific note — “the LCM should be 12” — is worth more to us than a page of general comment.

Who is reviewing

Recorded alongside your decisions so the sign-off is attributable. We will not publish your address.
